\section{自由振動解析解}

以下為該理論在四邊簡支（SSSS）邊界條件下，自由振動控制方程退化為代數矩陣的精確數學表達式：
位移場與轉角場假設（Navier Boundary Conditions）
$$w(x,y,t) = W_{mn} \sin(\alpha_m x) \sin(\beta_n y) e^{i\omega t}$$
$$\psi_x(x,y,t) = X_{mn} \cos(\alpha_m x) \sin(\beta_n y) e^{i\omega t}$$
$$\psi_y(x,y,t) = Y_{mn} \sin(\alpha_m x) \cos(\beta_n y) e^{i\omega t}$$
其中 $\alpha_m = \frac{m\pi}{a}$，$\beta_n = \frac{n\pi}{b}$。
廣義特徵值矩陣方程（Generalized Eigenvalue Problem）
$$\begin{bmatrix} K_{11} & K_{12} & K_{13} \\ K_{21} & K_{22} & K_{23} \\ K_{31} & K_{32} & K_{33} \end{bmatrix} \begin{Bmatrix} W_{mn} \\ X_{mn} \\ Y_{mn} \end{Bmatrix} = \omega^2 \begin{bmatrix} M_{11} & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & M_{22} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & M_{33} \end{bmatrix} \begin{Bmatrix} W_{mn} \\ X_{mn} \\ Y_{mn} \end{Bmatrix}$$
剛度矩陣元（Stiffness Matrix Elements）
$$K_{11} = D^s (\alpha_m^2 + \beta_n^2)$$
$$K_{12} = D^s \alpha_m$$
$$K_{13} = D^s \beta_n$$
$$K_{21} = D^s \alpha_m$$
$$K_{22} = D \alpha_m^2 + D \left(\frac{1-\nu}{2}\right) \beta_n^2 + D^s$$
$$K_{23} = D \left(\frac{1+\nu}{2}\right) \alpha_m \beta_n$$
$$K_{31} = D^s \beta_n$$
$$K_{32} = D \left(\frac{1+\nu}{2}\right) \alpha_m \beta_n$$
$$K_{33} = D \beta_n^2 + D \left(\frac{1-\nu}{2}\right) \alpha_m^2 + D^s$$
質量矩陣元（Mass Matrix Elements）
$$M_{11} = I_0 = \rho h$$
$$M_{22} = I_2 = \frac{\rho h^3}{12}$$
$$M_{33} = I_2 = \frac{\rho h^3}{12}$$
材料與幾何常數定義
\begin{itemize}
    \item 彎曲剛度：$D = \frac{Eh^3}{12(1-\nu^2)}$
    \item 剪切剛度：$D^s = \kappa^2 G h = \frac{5}{6} \frac{E}{2(1+\nu)} h$
\end{itemize}
