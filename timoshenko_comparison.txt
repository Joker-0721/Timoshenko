Note: SS = Simply-supported, CC = Clamped
Analytical formulas:
- Simply-supported: w(x) = PL^4/(24D)(x/L - 2x^3/L^3 + x^4/L^4) + PL^2/(2S)(x/L - x^3/L^3)
- Cantilever: w(x) = PL^4/(24D)(6x/L - 4x^3/L^3 + x^4/L^4) + PL^2/(2S)(2x/L - x^2/L^2)

where D = Eh^3/12(1-ν^2) is flexural stiffness, S = kGA is shear stiffness

4. JULA IMPLEMENTATION RESULTS
================================================================================

The Julia implementation uses:
- E = 210 GPa (modulus of elasticity)
- G = 84 GPa (shear modulus) 
- A = 0.02 m² (cross-section area)
- I = 10e-5 m⁴ (moment of inertia)
- κ = 5/6 (shear correction factor)
- L = 10 m (length)
- q = 1000 N/m (uniform load)

Expected results from analytical formulas:
- Simply-supported: w_max = 5qL⁴/(384EI) = 5 * 1000 * 10⁴ / (384 * 2.1e11 * 1e-4)
  = 0.0620 m (without shear)
- Clamped: w_max = qL⁴/(384EI) = 0.0124 m (without shear)

With shear deformation (Timoshenko), results should be slightly larger.
